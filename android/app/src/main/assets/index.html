    On-device AI

    Artha reads your bank SMS with Qwen 2.5 1.5B, a small AI model that runs entirely on this phone. Your messages never leave it.

    AI model

    The download is a one-time file of about 1.6 GB, so use Wi-Fi. A safety check makes sure OTPs, failed payments and scheduled AutoPay messages are never counted as spending.
